## What was changed

Each of the 23 original SVG files was drawn as a PNG with a transparent
background at 128, 256 and 384 pixels square (`2.0x/` and `3.0x/` hold the
larger sizes), with the empty margin trimmed and the picture centred with 6%
padding. The drawings themselves were not changed. The original SVG files are
kept in `docs/brand/mulberry/` at the top of the repository.

These PNG files are shared under the same licence, CC BY-SA 4.0.
